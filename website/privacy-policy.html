Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

We at Lariv ("we," "our," "us") are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI integration services, software, and platforms (the “Services”).

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lariv. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your data. Lariv is a brand owned by Uniquity Ventures, located at 302-Bonanza Arcade, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053. By using our ERP and AI services, you agree to these terms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Client Data: This is the business data you upload to the Lariv ERP. It includes documents, code, financial records, and datasets. You own this data. We only use it to provide our services to you.

  • Personal Data: This is information that identifies you. It includes your name, email address, and phone number.

  • Platform Data: Since we are a Meta Tech Provider, we may process data from Meta platforms. This includes information from WhatsApp Business API or Facebook accounts that you connect to Lariv.

  • Technical Data: Our servers automatically record your IP address, browser type, and how you use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to make the Lariv ERP work for you.

For Client Data and Platform Data, we use it only to build and run your custom AI solutions. We do not use your business data to train our own general AI models. We will never sell your data to other companies.

For Personal Data, we use it to manage your account and talk to you about our services.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not trade or rent your data. We only share data with:

  • Service Providers: We use trusted companies for cloud hosting (like AWS) and payments. They must keep your data secret and safe.

  • Legal Reasons: We may share data if the law in India requires us to do so.

  • Meta Compliance: We share data with Meta only as required to run the integrations you have asked for.

5. Data Security and Storage

We take security seriously. We use SSL encryption when data moves between your computer and our servers. We also encrypt your passwords when they are stored.

We keep your personal data as long as your account is active. We keep your business data for as long as our contract lasts. If you close your account, we delete your data from our systems.

6. Your Rights

Under Indian law, you have the following rights:

  • Access: You can ask for a copy of your data.

  • Correction: You can ask us to fix wrong information.

  • Deletion: You can ask us to delete your data.

  • Withdraw Consent: You can tell us to stop processing your data at any time.

To use these rights, email us at support@lariv.in.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Lariv account and associated personal data at any time.

To delete your account:

  1. Send an email to support@lariv.in from the email address associated with your account.

  2. Use the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.

  3. Include your full name and any workspace or organization details that help us identify your account.

Once we verify your request, we will delete your personal data and account information from our systems. Business (Client) data tied to an active contract may be retained until that contract ends, then removed. Some information may be kept where Indian law requires us to retain it.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when the process is finished.

8. Cookies

We only use Essential Cookies. These are small files that help our website work correctly. We do not use cookies for tracking or showing you ads.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will email you. We will also update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or account deletion can be sent to support@lariv.in.
